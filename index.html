My story

The day ChatGPT launched, I went all in on AI creativity. I haven't stopped since.

I've watched every wave up close: the first chatbots, the image models, the video models, and now agents. I don't just read about the updates. I open them on day one, test them, and use them in my work every single day.

When a tool I need doesn't exist, I build it. That's how FinalSay and Agent Pays Agent started. Everything I learn turns into content, so the people who follow me get the honest version: what works, what breaks, and how to make something good with it.
